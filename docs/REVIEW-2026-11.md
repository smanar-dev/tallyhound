# Platform review, November 2026

Three reviews of the email-first product after the fetch-list build: a code and rules review against CLAUDE.md, a hands-on QA pass through the running app (desktop, 390px phone, script on and off, keyboard, two tabs, double clicks), and a user-experience review against docs/UX.md and docs/REBRAND.md with screenshots of every screen. Everything below marked fixed has a test.

## Fixed

### Rules and data (code review)
- The check-in could reach a client whose fetch list nobody approved, and after a week it approved the list by itself. Email-first firms no longer send it, and an old one never confirms a list.
- An unsubscribed or bounced client could still be emailed through an approved draft. Drafts are discarded, approving one is refused, the timer drops held ones, and `claim()` never sends to the address.
- A contact's upload key and every unsubscribe key reached the browser in `/api/state`. They no longer do.
- A contact asking to stop was never acted on; their replies and bounces were not matched. They are now, and unsubscribing is by address across clients and contacts, with a person told.
- The "Anything new" name skipped `scrub`. It goes through it, and a name that held an identifier is not kept.
- Numbers split by spaces ("123 45 6789") passed the four-digit rule. They count as one number in the engine and the reader; "W-2 2025" still reads.
- A contact assignment was lost on a list rebuild; Not this season clients did not roll forward; a template or check-in answers confirmed a list without approval. All fixed.

### Behaviour (QA)
- An SSN or birth date under a header like "Tax ID" or "Born" was shown and kept for 30 days. A dashed SSN or a column of old dates is now sensitive whatever the header, and any SSN typed into another cell is scrubbed.
- A new address after an unsubscribe did nothing; any text was accepted as an email. `setEmail` checks, lower-cases, restarts letters for a new address and refuses one that asked to stop.
- A stop from a client who had gone quiet left the "no reply" item open. It closes, and can't reach opens.
- A W-2 for someone else was filed as a duplicate of the client's own. It goes to a person.
- A contact's first email was worded as a reminder about "your" return, and assigning an item sent the client an extra letter. Fixed.
- Two tabs confirming one import showed a raw error; a double click replaced a success with an error; the import file picker could not be reached by keyboard; Progress scrolled sideways on a phone. Fixed.
- A file type that cannot be opened left the upload page "checking" for good. It is refused at once, in words.
- "Asked 0 times" after several letters; numbers accepted in catalog wording and payer names; letter wording could link to another site or ask for an SSN or payment; accented and lower-case names; empty imports. Fixed.

### Experience (UX)
- A suggested step showed as already done; a document waiting for a person showed as accepted and counted as verified. Both now say they wait.
- The firm's public page talked about texting. Email-first firms get an email-only page.
- Phone layouts (fetch list rows, client list status, queue rows), one "waiting on you" count, one orange action per view, a shorter document check panel, tabs that fit, fetch list and approve words throughout, a status pill that agrees with the list, a stopped-emails card that says to call, honest sending-hours words, barks instead of alerts, plain import reasons.
- Replies by email open with the client's name and are signed; one document is "Upload it here"; last year's return is asked for by the right year.
- The upload page: an upload that is not on the list says so and what is still needed; a received item comes off the list with script; one clearly labelled place the files wait; 44px tap targets.

## Not done, and why
- **Upload page without script.** A file chosen with a button beside an item shows only in the "Files to send" box further down. Per-item forms would fix it but change the page every test and the progress script rely on. Worth doing before a wide rollout, with a session on a real phone.
- **Form names in letters.** The review suggested "Mortgage interest statement (Form 1098) from Hillcrest Mortgage" everywhere. That is a wording choice for the pilot firms, not a defect: owners can already change it per document in Settings, Documents.
- **Settings, Checklists.** Kept and renamed "Starter lists" rather than hidden: a new client with no records still starts from it, and it is the only place to edit it.
- **"Send files to preparer"** shows once a document has passed, even while the pilot still waits for a person to confirm it. Harmless (the firm's own files), but it may read as early.
- **Duplicate emails** between two clients are allowed on the Details tab, because spouses often share one.
